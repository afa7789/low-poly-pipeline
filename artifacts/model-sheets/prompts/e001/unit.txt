# redesign: The line every other archer inherits from: conical oiled-canvas hood with short ear flaps, canvas cape falling to two points at the knee, and one compact moon bow whose hollow crescent upper limb sits over a straight lower limb, carried low and tilted off the vertical. Separation from the Elf archers is carried by posture and material, never by finish: she stands in a hunter's forward lean with weight on the ball of the front foot and the bow cutting the torso diagonally, dressed in laced rawhide and borrowed plate scrap with no tailored apron, no front triangle, no level and no plumb line anywhere on her. Savagery shows as method: an open jaw trap hangs at the belt, since that is how the line takes veins at night, and a tooth toggle closes the throat. Four props only: the moon bow, one closed sheaf carried as a slab instead of a row of countable arrows, two emergency shafts crossed on the chest because the cheapest unit carries no large quiver by doctrine, and the dead lantern hooked on the hip opposite the bow as ritual asymmetry and humanoid counterweight. Pose: bow half raised, short silent step; idle has the cape flapping against ground fog. Story: the youngest in the race — fog is their shooting school, and none of them looses before three counted breaths, which is what Fleetfooted looks like from outside. Budget: 300–800 tris (~350), 1 atlas, no transparency except glass.
