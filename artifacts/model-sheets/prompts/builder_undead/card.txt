This sheet shows the Undead Builder: one gaunt hooded worker in a pale bone-coloured patched robe cut at the knee, a brown cloth tied over the lower face, a number stencilled on the chest, a wooden crate hanging on a single strap at the right hip with a green stamp, an iron splint on the left shin, thin legs in puttees and boots, a long hooked pry bar in the right hand.
