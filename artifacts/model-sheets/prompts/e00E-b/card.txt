This sheet shows ONLY the Weeper, the short wide heavy child of the Chaos Twins pair: a soot-black body, a dirty-ivory porcelain weeping mask with two dark tear streaks and an ember seam band around its rim, a spoked valve wheel held in the left hand. No other figure, no ledger, no gas cistern. The left panel is a stand-in (the parent sheet had no profile of this figure).
