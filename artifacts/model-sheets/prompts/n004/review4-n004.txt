Changes to the creature:
1. Smaller head: shrink the head to about half its current size, so it is clearly narrower than the chest and in normal game proportions, not big-headed or chibi. Replace the heavy, frowning, frog-like brow and wide grin with plain lizard features: one small glowing pale-turquoise eye on each side of the head. Scale the two ivory horns and the teeth down with the head.
2. More lizard-like body: make the chest and shoulders slimmer and the body long, low and flat, with the belly slung just above the ground. Sprawl all four legs out to the sides with bent elbows and knees, as a monitor lizard stands, instead of standing upright under the body. Keep all four legs and the clawed feet. The long tapering tail stays and now lies level with the body.
3. Spitter: add one swollen throat sac under the jaw in acid yellow-green, with the mouth slightly open and the same acid yellow-green inside. It is part of the creature's flesh, not a device. Do not draw a flying jet or puddle of spit.
