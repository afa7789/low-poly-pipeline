Dieselpunk for armed humanoids (author references, Trench Crusade and classic dieselpunk):
Weapons: bolt-action rifle with bayonet, flamethrower, spiked trench club, pump shotgun, water-cooled heavy machine gun, gas grenades, two-handed crusader sword, heavy revolver, battle axe, relic weapon.
Clothes and armour: gas mask fused with a knight's visor or plague beak, long worn muddy greatcoat, steel Stahlhelm or Brodie helmet mixed with a greathelm and cross eye slit, chainmail and steel pauldrons over an olive or grey military tunic, cross tabard, wax purity seals with scrolls, puttees over leather boots, crossed ammunition bandoliers, thick leather apron, primitive spring and cable prosthetic.
Pick two or three of these per unit, as large crude shapes. Fantasy races keep their own body.

Never a fuel tank or gas tank on the back. Dieselpunk means combustion engines, dirty lead-acid electricity, 1920s to 1940s noir, trench artillery and classic aviation.
More weapons: motor-cleaver (butcher blade with a small two-stroke engine in the guard and an exhaust at the pommel), riveted double-barrel piston shotgun, arc-welding mace (forked electrodes, thick battery in the handle), coilgun in a Tommy Gun silhouette with copper coils and a round capacitor drum, oversized hand cannon fed by a short ammunition belt with a ring sight.
More looks: art-deco enforcer (pinstripe suit, heavy dark coat, brass deco pauldrons, sooty fedora, chest holster), scrap pilot (shearling bomber jacket, mechanic overalls tied at the waist, aviator goggles on the forehead, heavy rubber gloves), U-boat raider (mustard or moss waxed slicker, roll-neck sweater, high rubber boots, valve tools on the belt, giant dive watch), trench runner (muddy greatcoat, puttees, Brodie helmet with a bolted lens lamp, gas mask hanging at the hip), grease-pit brawler (high-waist trousers, canvas suspenders, rolled sleeves, flat cap, taped knuckles).
