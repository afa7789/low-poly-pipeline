Edit this model sheet. Change only the quills and the edge of the back shell, and make the same change in all five views. The goal is quills that clearly read as projectiles this creature can throw, and a slightly more alien, chaotic creature.

CHANGE 1, the quills: remove the two long curved red horn-like spikes from the back completely. Replace them with six separate quills growing out of the orange shell, three on each side of the spine, in two mirrored rows.
