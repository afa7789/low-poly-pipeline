# redesign: The keeper of the moon count: same column as the Huntress, one step of rank expressed as geometry rather than trim, so she is taller, hood higher, and her bow is strongly asymmetric with the crescent sitting off-centre above the shoulder line and the lower limb shortened to a stub, a shape no straight Elf bow can be confused with. The multi-target read survives 32px because the extra shots are form and not tally: the lower limb ends in a split forked nock holding two splayed shafts as one mass, so +2 targets is a three-pronged fork rather than three small arrows nobody can count. The cape changes shape too, going to a single cape mass pushed entirely to one side, breaking the column's symmetry and giving her a broader, off-axis read than any tailored Elf front. Materials carry the rank honestly: no added finery, only heavier material, a shoulder of overlapped plate scrap cut into scales from salvaged sheeting and, at the hip, a sealed glass reliquary of charged salt that must be refilled under a full moon, the physical license for the extra shafts. Pose: she draws in profile and steps sideways after loosing, never retreating straight back, which keeps her read distinct from both the Huntress and any square-stanced Elf shooter. Story: rangers keep the count of moons, every extra shaft on the string is a phase earned, and she refuses to fight under a sky that gives her nothing to charge by. Budget: 300–800 tris (~420), 1 atlas, transparency only for the reliquary glass.
