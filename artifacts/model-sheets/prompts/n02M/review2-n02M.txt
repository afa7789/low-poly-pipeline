The goal is to fix the colours, add three dieselpunk clothing pieces, and clear the back. The result must read as the same goblin as its predecessor, a sapper with a slate-blue Brodie helmet, a maroon leather apron and khaki puttees, now upgraded with a rocket rack.

CHANGES
1. Remove the wooden crate backpack completely. Nothing replaces it: no tank, no box, no pack, no cylinder on the back. Two wide dark-brown leather straps run over the shoulders and hold the rack against the upper back.
2. Remove the brass spotting scope and its tube completely.
3. Add a wide-brimmed steel Brodie helmet in flat slate blue (#7388A0) on the head, as one large simple shape, sitting just above the goggles.
4. Replace the bright red vest with a long, thick leather apron in dark maroon (#5E2A2C), reaching the knees, worn over a plain charcoal tunic (#3A3A40).
5. Change the black trousers to slate grey (#55585F). Wrap the lower legs in khaki puttees (#B5A67C) as a few broad flat bands above dark charcoal boots.
