Five orthographic drawings of the same unit — a Mech Heavy Tank (id h06S) from a dieselpunk dark-fantasy low-poly game — one drawing per panel in a five-panel layout: a top row of three panels (front, left profile, back) and a bottom row of two panels (top-down, bottom-up), with wide empty margins between them. THE SUBJECT: a fat, toy-like, cartoonish war tank in the spirit of the chunky Warcraft 3 vehicles, with exaggerated proportions: short and very wide, huge rounded wheels, an oversized turret, a stubby fat cannon, a big brass beast snout on the front. It should look funny, tough and heavy, like a bulldog made of brass and iron. Every corner is slightly rounded and swollen; there are no thin, long or realistic military shapes.

THIS IS THE SAME CHARACTER AS THE PREDECESSOR SHEET, UPGRADED. What stays: the black wedge-shaped hull with brass edging, the big brass-framed plough at the front, the round brass-ringed turret with a brass-tipped cannon, the two spoked wheels on each side at the rear, the red side panel in front of the wheels, the two rear exhaust stacks in steel grey with black tips, and one copper pipe run along the flank. What grows: the whole tank is about a third bigger and much fatter, the hull is taller and wider, the wheels are about twice as thick and have four fat spokes, and the turret and cannon are doubled in thickness and shortened. The thin plough becomes a massive brass prow shaped like a blunt beast snout, with two glowing blue eyes and three short black spikes along its lower edge. The several thin copper pipes merge into one fat copper pipe. The stacks become short and fat and flare outward into black cones, without smoke.
