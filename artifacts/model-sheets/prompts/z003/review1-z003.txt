The subject: the goblin flamegunner from the reference sheet, promoted to sergeant. He is unmistakably the same goblin, but now a big armoured war boss in heavy red plate, with his bare green face showing and a tall spiked crown of rank. It must read at a glance as the officer who commands the small flamegunners.

What stays from the reference sheet: the same goblin with the same long, pointed green ears sticking out to the sides, the same stocky build with short thick legs and big boots, the same brass goggles with yellow lenses (now pushed up onto the helmet band), the same brown glove on the right hand, the same yellow pilot-flame drop at the weapon's muzzle.

What grows and changes: the body is about a fifth taller and nearly twice as wide across the shoulders, with a thick chest and heavy arms. The black mask is gone and the face is bare. The big back tank, the hose coil, the back plate, the straps and the belt pouch are gone: nothing is worn on the back. The small torch is replaced by one huge gun.

The body, top to bottom:
