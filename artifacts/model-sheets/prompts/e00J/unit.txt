# redesign: The elder who interrogates the buried blood: his mantle is woven root and spoil-heavy earth together, lichen and mud in the same cloth, and his crown is a ring of crooked silver nails pulled from industrial fencing, worn as a ritual insignia with no antlers and no foliage anywhere, since every plant shape belongs to Nature. The area read replaces the old detail work: behind his shoulders sits a triple coil of root threaded through barbed wire, three distinct loops as one large mass rather than three small seeds, and the cast animation plants the staff and opens three ground hooks at once, which is what 3 targets and 5 seconds look like when they have to be understood before impact. The face stays sealed, closing the breach the earlier draft left open: the old beard becomes a veil of plant fibre hanging from a featureless bone-plaster mask, with the nail crown mounted over the hood above it, so nothing requires a chin or an exposed mouth. The trade is on him too: a moon-count tally rod of notched silver at the belt, each notch a stopped pump, because the race keeps its records on sticks and not in books, and three iron seed burrs are gone in favour of the triple coil they were always meant to be. Pose: he plants the great crescent scythe-staff with both hands like a stake driven into a rail, then opens his arms; idle is a slow bend as if listening to something under the plates. Story: he speaks with roots and not with machines, and the machines he silences never learn his name. Budget: 300–800 tris (~500), 1 atlas, no transparency except glass.
